Privacy Policy

Link To QR — Android app and this website. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

What Link To QR is

Link To QR is a tools app and website (QR makers, PDF and photo tools, a document scanner) with optional user accounts and an optional paid Premium plan. The same account works in the app and on this website.

Your files stay on your device

The PDF, photo and scanner tools work on your own phone/computer. The files you open in a tool are not uploaded to any server and we cannot see them. A file leaves your device only if you yourself download, share or send it somewhere.

Account information

If you create an account, we store your name, email address and account details (such as whether Premium is active and its expiry date) using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your plain password. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us for the account.

Premium payments

Premium is paid by UPI directly to the owner's UPI ID shown in the app/website. To start Premium, you submit the payment's UTR number and a screenshot of the successful payment. The screenshot is stored together with the payment request in our database and is visible only to the owner/admin for checking the payment. Payment requests and their status (pending / approved / rejected) are stored with your account.

Ads

The Android app may show ads provided by Google AdMob. This website may in future show ads provided by Google AdSense. These Google services may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not read the documents, photos or PDFs you process in the tools.
  • We do not ask for your bank, card or UPI PIN details — UPI payments happen inside your own UPI app.

Data safety and deletion

Account data is protected by Firebase security rules: only the admin can change Premium status or ban an account, and payment screenshots can be read only by the admin. If you want your account and its data deleted, contact us at the email below and we will delete it.

Children

Link To QR is a general-purpose tools service and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
