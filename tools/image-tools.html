🖼️ Image Tools

All photos are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

JPG to PDF

Add photos, drag them into order, and get one PDF. Each photo becomes one page.

    Image Compress

    Makes the photo file smaller. Lower quality = smaller file.

    Image Resize

    Change the photo's pixel size — type pixels, or just give a percent.

    Photo Size — Target KB

    Shrink a photo to fit under a target size (handy for online forms), or grow a small photo up to a minimum size.

    HD keeps the full original resolution and only lowers quality to reach the target. If even the lowest quality is too big at full resolution, HD will say so honestly — use Normal then.

    Format Converter

    Change a photo between JPG, PNG and WebP.

    Crop Photo

    Drag the green box to choose the part you keep. Drag its corners to resize it.

    Photo Editor

    A normal photo editor: adjust the light, use a filter, draw, and add your own text. It does not change text that is already inside the photo.

    Note: rotating or flipping starts the drawing again (adjustments and filter are kept).

    Passport Size Photo Maker

    One photo in — a print-ready sheet of passport-size copies (35 × 45 mm) out. Drag the green box over the face. The crop box is always the upright 35 × 45 passport shape — a wide photo is centre-cropped into it. The Paper setting below decides how the copies sit on the sheet (on Landscape paper they turn sideways on the sheet, studio style).

    Your crop always stays 35 × 45 upright — this setting is only about the paper. On Landscape paper the sheet turns wide and each photo turns sideways on the sheet to fit it, like studio sheets.

    Normal sheet is 150 DPI (fine for screen sharing). HD sheet is 300 DPI — the quality print shops ask for. Face size and sheet size are the same in both.
