📷 Scanner

All photos are processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Document Scanner

Take a photo of a paper or pick one from the gallery. Set the corners, clean the page up, add more pages if you like, then download one PDF.

STEP 1 · SET THE CORNERS OF THE PAGE

Auto-adjust runs by itself on every photo: the four green circles are fitted to the corners of the paper, so the background is left out of the final scan. Drag any circle to fix it by hand.

STEP 2 · CLEAN IT UP

Reading text runs on your device (English). A page with big, clear letters reads best. To save this page as a PDF instead: tap “Add This Page” below, then “Download PDF”.

Drag the blue line to where the two pages join (the middle fold), then split. The left page is added first, then the right page — reading order.

Changing the filter starts again from the clean scan, so markup and a stamped signature are removed — add them last.

YOUR PAGES

    Each page also has its own JPG download button. The PDF page size is set at 150 DPI, like a normal scan.

    QR Scanner — Scan to Open

    Point the camera at a QR code, or pick a photo of one. If the code holds a link, you can open it straight away. If it holds plain text, you can copy it. If the live camera does not work in the app, picking a photo of the QR code works just as well.
